Never leave the last pictures in silence

When the music is shorter than the slideshow, the pictures now fit it by themselves — down to ⅔ of their length, never below 2 s — so the slideshow ends with the music. When even that would still leave silence, a note offers two one-tap fixes: repeat the music, or add a song. A music longer than the slideshow is unchanged.

What's decided

  • Pictures using their own seconds or a mood's length share a shorter music by themselves, down to ⅔ of their length, never below 2 s — the slideshow ends with the music.
  • If even that would still leave silence, the pictures keep their length and a note offers two one-tap fixes: Repeat the music or Add a song.
  • Repeating plays the whole playlist again from its first song, crossfading like between songs, for as long as the slideshow lasts — the last song fades out with the last picture.
  • A music longer than the slideshow is unchanged: it fades out with the last picture.
