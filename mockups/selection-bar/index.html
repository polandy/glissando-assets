Selection bar — icons with label bubbles

One working slideshow. Tap a picture to select it, then use the bar: Earlier and Later really move it, Remove really removes it (with Undo), Insert chapter really adds a chapter, View and Edit open. No menu: every action stays one tap away as an icon, and its word lives in a bubble. The variants differ only in when the bubbles show — switch between them to compare; your slideshow stays as it is across variants. Generated colour tiles, no photos.
