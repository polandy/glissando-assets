Slim selection bar

Today a single selected picture shows seven controls, the bookmark for “Insert chapter” says nothing on a phone, and on a desktop the selected tile carries its own ✕ next to the bar’s Remove. Both proposals keep the everyday actions — Earlier, Later, Edit, Remove, Done — in the bar and move the rare ones behind ⋯. Tap a tile to select it, tap ⋯ to open the menu; on the desktop frames hover a tile to see its ✕.
