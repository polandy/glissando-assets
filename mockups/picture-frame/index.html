Picture frame mode

An endless, varied slideshow from an Immich album for a tablet or TV browser on the wall. Set up once, then it just runs: pictures shuffle in rounds, Ken Burns and transitions vary on their own, the screen stays awake. Pictures here are generated gradients.

1 · Start screen and set-up (needs the Glissando server with Immich)

A "Picture frame" card sits beside the slideshows on the start screen. It opens one screen: pick an album, choose the pace, start. The choice is remembered on this device; "Copy frame link" gives a link that starts the same frame directly, to bookmark on the tablet.

2 · The running frame (tablet, landscape)

No controls on screen. A tap shows a small bar (Pause, Next, Exit) and the ✕/Exit corner for 4 s, then everything fades again. Back gesture or Esc exits. The date and place of the picture sit quietly bottom left; the clock is optional. Tap the stage.
