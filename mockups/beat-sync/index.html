How it works

  • Detection on the device, once per music file, in the background when its slideshow opens: the track is decoded, an onset curve is built, the tempo is found by autocorrelation (60–200 BPM) and the beat grid's phase by a comb over the whole track. Stored with the music as a steady grid — first beat and beat length — so it travels in the .glissando file.
  • On the beat is the default once a beat is found. Each automatic picture change moves to the nearest beat (never below the 2-second minimum); a crossfade lasts a whole number of beats, so it starts on a beat and lands on one.
  • Own durations stay exact — a picture with its own duration keeps it; the next automatic change snaps again. The slideshow still ends with the music.
  • No steady beat (speech, ambient, rubato): the section says so and the pictures share the music evenly, as before.
  • The same timing plays everywhere: player, video export, web page export.
Decided: own detector (no dependency), a constant-tempo grid rather than a list of beats, "On the beat" on by default. See ADR-0021 in the PR.
