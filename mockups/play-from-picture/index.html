Play from a chosen picture

Mockup. Pick a picture, then start the show from it instead of from the beginning. The three entry points run in the same app: switch between them and try each. All pictures are generated.

Selection bar: "Play from here" shows only with exactly one picture selected. Viewer: the bottom bar. Editor: the header. When the show ends, or on Esc or close, you land back on the screen you started from; the selection is kept. The show crossfades every 2.5 s; the music indicator is offset to the starting point and fades in over 1 s.
