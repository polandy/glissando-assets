Move several pictures together

Select more than one tile and move them as a group: with the selection bar, by dragging (mouse), or with the keyboard. Try both views. All pictures here are generated.

    Selecting several

    • A tap or click still selects one picture, as now.
    • Select beside "Add pictures" starts selecting several: every tile gets a circle and a tap or click adds or removes it.
    • Touch: hold a tile to start the same way (the hint says "hold to select several"). It lifts with a short buzz.
    • Mouse: Ctrl/⌘+click adds or removes; Shift+click adds the range from the last picked one.
    • The bar then shows the count ("3 selected", on the phone too) instead of "Picture 3 of 12"; Edit appears only while one is selected. Done or Esc ends it.

    Moving the group

    • Earlier / Later move all selected pictures one step; they stay selected.
    • Drag any selected tile: the whole group follows, shown as a stack with its count, and lands together at the dashed line, in its play order. Dragging an unselected tile moves only that one.
    • Remove removes the whole group; the toast "3 pictures removed" undoes all of it. At least one picture stays.
    • Touch: hold and move a tile to drag the selection with it (the held tile joins it). Near the top or bottom edge the strip scrolls along. Lifting the finger in place only selects.

    Keyboard

    • Ctrl+Space on a focused tile starts selecting several and adds it.
    • While selecting several: arrows move only the focus, Space/Enter add or remove, Shift+Space adds the range.
    • Shift+arrows move the whole group (up and down by a row); Delete removes it; Esc deselects all.
