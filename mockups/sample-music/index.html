Sample music, second round

The tabs hid the sample music behind a second tab, and adding a track there changed what the other tab showed. This round drops the tabs: one soundtrack holds every chosen song, wherever it came from, and the sample music is the step's centrepiece.

  • A — one page (recommended). Soundtrack on top, then “Music for your slideshow” as a cover grid with mood chips, then a slim “Your own music” row. Adding is the + on a cover; the song appears in the soundtrack right above, so a tap's effect shows where it happens.
  • B — two doors. The step stays short: a large “Music for your slideshow” card with a cover mosaic and a slim “Your own music” row; the first opens the grid as a sheet. The soundtrack sits below both.
  • The music editor's “Add a song” opens the same picker as variant B's sheet, in both variants.
