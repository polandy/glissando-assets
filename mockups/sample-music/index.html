Sample music

In the music step of a new slideshow (and behind “Add a song” in the music editor), a second tab offers nine tracks that come with Glissando. Tap a cover to listen, tap “Add” to put the track into the playlist. Try both layouts, the dark theme and the offline state.

How it works

  • Listen first. Tapping a cover streams the track from the Glissando site; only one plays at a time, tapping again pauses. Nothing is stored for a preview.
  • Add means download. “Add” downloads the track once and stores it in the slideshow like your own music, so the trim, fades, volume and beat changes work as usual, and every export (.glissando file, web page, video) carries it.
  • Offline. Without a connection, previews and new downloads are not possible; tracks already in a slideshow on this device stay available and are marked “On this device”.
  • Credit. The tracks are CC BY-NC 4.0: the picker names the licence, and an exported web page and video carry the credit line.
  • Size. Nothing is bundled in the app; the nine tracks are fetched only when picked.
