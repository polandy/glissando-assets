Trip chapters

A short slide wherever the trip moves on to a new place. Consecutive days spent near the same place form one stay, and a stay gets one chapter, at its start, named after the place — two weeks in one spot is still a single chapter, while a day trip in between gets its own. Without location data there is nothing to name a chapter after, so it has to be added by hand. Use the bar below to try phone and desktop sizes, both themes, and the states that change what you see.

Player — chapter slide

The day's first picture, darkened, with the title in the lower third — the same card as the opening slide, just quieter. Pick a chip to preview a multi-day stay, a single-day trip, a manual chapter with no GPS to name it, or German UI text. Play runs the real sequence: previous picture → chapter → next picture.

Editor — picture strip

A slimmer tile sits before a stay's first picture. Tap an existing chapter to open its sheet: the automatic text is editable, a link resets it, and an automatic chapter can be hidden on its own. Tap a picture instead to select it, then insert a chapter by hand at that point. Turn automatic chapters off for the whole slideshow from the same sheet — chapters added by hand stay either way.
