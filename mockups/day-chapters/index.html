Day chapters

A short slide before each new day of a trip, named after the day's most common place. On by default for any slideshow spanning two or more calendar days; off while the picture order is arranged by hand. Use the bar below to try phone and desktop sizes, both themes, and the two states that turn chapters off.

Player — two looks

Same card style as the title slide, just quieter. Pick the chips to see a chapter with a place, without GPS, and in German. Play runs the real sequence: previous picture → chapter → next picture.

Editor — picture strip

A slimmer tile sits between the days. Tap one to open its sheet: the automatic text is editable, a link resets it, and each chapter can be hidden on its own. Turn chapters off for the whole slideshow from the same sheet.
