Pace comes from the mood, not a picture-length choice

The music step no longer asks how long each picture should last. A new slideshow always starts on the mood Calm — about 7 s per picture, paced to the music's phrases — and that stays changeable later, in the slideshow itself.

  • Removed: the "Picture length" choice (About 5 s each / Fill the music) and its stepper, and — without music — the "Time per picture" card and stepper.
  • In their place: one quiet line stating the mood and its pace, with a pointer to where it can still be changed.
  • Unchanged: the thumbnail strip, the fit notes (fits / too short / repeats / music longer) and the "Pictures · per picture · Total" row — now reading Calm's 7 s.

What's decided

  • A new slideshow always starts on the mood Calm — about 7 s per picture, on the music's phrases — set once, not asked for during import.
  • The music step states this in a single quiet line instead of offering a choice; the mood stays changeable afterwards, in the slideshow's own Details panel.
  • The shrink-to-fit rule is unchanged: pictures sharing a shorter music still fit it by themselves, down to ⅔ of their length, never below 2 s.
  • If even that would leave silence, the note still offers Repeat the music or Add a song, exactly as today.
