Shuffle the pictures

Mockup. The owner mixes the pictures of a slideshow into a random order with one tap on the slideshow screen. Click Shuffle in the app frame below.

What it does

A Shuffle button sits in the strip head, beside Select and Add pictures. One tap puts every picture of the slideshow into a new random order. The order is stored like any manual reorder, so the player, the HTML export and the video all use it, and the order line then reads "Own order".

How the shuffle shows

  • The tiles glide to their new places (about half a second) and their number badges change; each tile that moved gets a short accent outline. With reduced motion they jump instead.
  • The Shuffle icon rolls once, and the order line says Shuffled with an accent dot until the next edit.
  • Undo brings the previous order back. Tapping Shuffle again draws a new order — it never returns the order it started from.

Variants

A · Toast with Undo. Same pattern as removing a picture: a toast "Shuffled 12 pictures" with Undo. Consistent with the app's other undos.
B · Undo in the order line. No toast; the order line becomes "Shuffled · Undo". Quieter, but the undo sits at the top and is easy to miss on a phone.
Open questions. Is a one-time reshuffle (the order is then fixed) what you want, or a "play in random order" setting that reshuffles on every play? This mockup assumes the former — it is visible, undoable and what you see is what plays. With a selection active, shuffle could mix only the selected pictures; left out of this first step.
