Stop the title slide repeating its title

Today, a new title slide shows "July 2025" twice, and the player's top bar shows it a third time. The fix has two parts. A new title slide gets an empty subtitle while the slideshow keeps its automatic month title, and the day range once you have renamed the slideshow. On a title or closing slide, the top bar shows no heading. Pictures are generated gradients.
