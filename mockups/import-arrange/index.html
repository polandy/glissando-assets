Arrange while importing

Import wizard, step 1. Pick more at any time; each pick joins the running import as a row in the queue. Ready pictures can be selected, moved and removed exactly as in the slideshow editor while the rest keep coming. Tap a tile, try Add pictures during the import, stop a row with ✕. All pictures are generated.

How it behaves

  • Picking never waits. “Add pictures” and “From Immich” stay above the strip the whole time. Each pick becomes a row in the queue (“Lake trip · 6 / 9”, “3 pictures · waiting”); rows run one after another, one file at a time as today, so memory stays bounded.
  • Progress: one overall line with the mint meter (“14 of 26 ready”), and per row its own count. A placeholder tile per waiting item sits after the ready tiles; the one being read shows a running bar. Placeholders cannot be selected or moved.
  • Where new items land: while the order line reads “Capture order”, a ready item is sorted in by its date. The first move or shuffle switches it to “Own order”, and from then on new items go at the end, in capture order among themselves. Nothing you placed is shifted.
  • Arranging is the editor’s: tap selects (number badge in the accent), the inverse bar has Earlier, Later, View, Remove and Done; “Select” picks several and moves them as a group; with a mouse, drag a tile, ✕ on hover, Delete on a focused tile; “Shuffle” as in the editor. Remove shows “Picture removed · Undo”.
  • ✕ on a queue row drops that pick’s waiting items; the ones already in stay. A toast says so: “Stopped Lake trip · 6 pictures already in stay”.
  • Duplicates across picks are skipped before they are read, as today; the lemon notices add up across picks and keep “Add anyway”.
  • Next waits until the queue is empty and every picture is checked; the footer says why (“12 still importing”, “Checking 20 / 26 …”). Cancel and ← keep “Discard selection?”.
  • Culling runs once, after the queue is empty, as today. A picture you moved counts as chosen and is never set aside, the same as one taken in with +.
One decision for you
  • A tap now selects (as in the editor) instead of opening the picture full screen. Full screen moves to View in the selection bar, which stands where the editor has “Edit”; with a mouse a double-click also opens it. The picture editor itself stays in the slideshow editor, because it needs the slideshow. Alternative: keep tap = full screen and enter selection with a long press, which would make the wizard behave differently from the editor.
  • On touch the ✕ on each tile goes away (the editor has none on touch); Remove sits in the selection bar.
