On the TV, controlled from the phone

The TV only plays; everything you press happens on the phone. Needs the Glissando server (a slideshow stored there, ADR-0018), because the phone and the TV talk through it. Two ways in, both end on the same remote. The pictures are streamed to the TV from the server, never through the phone.

A · TV first: open the short address, pick on the phone (recommended)

On the TV's browser you type one short address, glissando.example/tv (a bookmark after the first time). The TV shows a QR code and a 4-letter code. On the phone, a new "Show on TV" button (on every server slideshow's screen) opens a sheet: scan the code with the camera, or type it. Then pick which slideshow to show. The phone you are signed in on is the owner's, so the whole library is on offer; the TV needs no sign-in, it only holds the code.

B · The TV already plays a shared link: "Control with your phone"

If the TV is already on a share link (/s/…, sent to the TV or typed), its start card and a corner chip show a small QR code. Scanning it opens the same remote for that slideshow, without signing in, for anyone in the room (the grandparents' TV, a guest's phone). No library, no slideshow choice: only the remote.

The remote and the TV while it plays

One remote for both ways in. Play and pause, previous and next picture, a scrubber across the whole slideshow, and the music volume. The screen stays on while the remote is open. "Disconnect" stops showing on the TV (the TV returns to its pairing screen); closing the remote or losing the connection leaves the TV playing. Try the buttons: the TV below follows.
