Remove a chosen picture in the pictures step

Import wizard, step 1. Every stored tile gets a ✕. One press removes the picture, without a question; a toast offers Undo. Try it in the frame below: click or tap a ✕, or press Tab to reach a ✕ and Enter, Space or Delete. The images are generated.

    How it behaves

    • Mouse: the ✕ shows when the pointer is over a tile. A click removes the picture.
    • Touch: the ✕ is always shown, 28 px drawn on a 44 px target. A tap removes the picture. There is no selection mode here, so nothing else is needed.
    • Keyboard: each ✕ is a button in the tab order, named "Remove picture from 4 Jul 18:00". Enter, Space, Delete or Backspace removes it. Focus then moves to the next tile's ✕, or the previous one at the end, or "Choose pictures" when none are left.
    • Undo: the toast "Picture removed" with Undo. Removals while it shows add up ("3 pictures removed"); one Undo puts them all back where they were. Next, Cancel or ← dismiss it, and the removed pictures are then dropped for good.
    • The count and date range follow at once. With every picture removed the drop zone comes back and Next is disabled, as at the start.
    • A tile still being downscaled has no ✕; it gets one once it is stored. Removing a picture also frees it from the duplicate check, so choosing it again takes it in.
    • "Add pictures" to an existing slideshow uses the same step and gets the same ✕.

    Considered and left out: tap to select, then a "Remove" bar, as in the slideshow editor. That costs two taps for one picture and adds a mode to a screen that has none. A confirm dialog is left out as well, because Undo covers a slip.
