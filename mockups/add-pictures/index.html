Add pictures to a slideshow

The slideshow view with 24 pictures. “Add pictures” opens the pictures step of New slideshow, just without the music step. The selection is simulated: “Choose pictures” delivers 10 generated pictures, 2 of which are already in the slideshow. All pictures are generated, no real photos.

How it works

  • Entry point: “Add pictures” in the header bar above the pictures, to the right of the count (variant A, proposal). It sits next to what it changes, and is visible on the phone without scrolling. B (a tile at the end of the pictures) ends up far down with 200 pictures, C (the info panel) crowds in next to “Play”. The selection bar gets no entry; it acts on the chosen pictures, not on new ones.
  • Same flow as the import: the pictures step of New slideshow, with the same drop zone, folder choice, Immich field, progress bar and notices. The music step falls away, the slideshow already has its music. The Immich picker (ADR-0013) is the same, just no longer tied to a new slideshow.
  • Downsizing and Ken Burns motion: exactly like the import, picture by picture on the device (PictureImport, at most 3840 × 2160). Photos from Immich bring their faces with them. For pictures from the device, the automatic focus pass then finds the people, as it does after the import.
  • Where the new pictures go: if the slideshow is sorted by capture date, they sort into their place, and the slideshow stays sorted. With your own order, they go to the end in capture order, and your order stays untouched. After that, they are marked “new” in the slideshow until you leave the view.
  • Duplicates: a picture already in the slideshow is skipped, and a notice names it. In the Immich picker, such photos are faded, marked “Already in” and not selectable. Anyone who wants a picture twice on purpose taps “Add anyway” in the notice.
  • Timing and music (ADR-0008): pictures with their own duration keep it. With music, all automatic pictures keep sharing the music, so each gets shorter, and the slideshow stays as long as the music. Below 2 s per picture is not possible. Then the slideshow gets longer than the music, and that shows before you add. Without music, each new picture gets the set seconds, and the slideshow gets longer. The “After adding” box shows this beforehand.
  • Undo: after adding, “10 pictures added · Undo” appears, as with removing. Undo takes exactly these pictures back out and frees their storage.
  • Cancel: “Cancel” or Back, while downsizing is still running, stops it and discards the new pictures already finished. The slideshow stays as it was. Only “Add 10” writes them into the slideshow.
  • Offline: pictures from the device always work. Immich needs the connection to the Glissando server, just like during the import.

Detecting duplicates: provenance instead of checksum (ADR-0016, proposal)

ApproachDetected byAssessment
Store provenance (proposal)Each picture remembers where it came from. For Immich that is the asset id. For a file it is the file name, size in bytes and capture time.Cheap, works without a secure context (an iPad over the LAN with http). Recognizes the same file from another device too. A renamed copy slips through.
Checksum of the original (SHA-256)Content of the original fileMost accurate. But needs crypto.subtle, so HTTPS, and has to read every original in full; noticeable with 200 HEIC photos. The picture from Immich and the same file from the device still count as different.
Checksum of the downsized picturePixels after downsizingDepends on the browser (decoder, colour profile), so not reliably the same.

Pictures from before this change have no stored provenance. For them: same file name and same capture time mean the same picture. Provenance travels along into the .glissando file.

Tests

  • Pure logic with unit tests: where new pictures go (by date or to the end), detecting duplicates (provenance, old pictures, duplicates within the same selection), undo. The “After adding” preview uses the same function as the player (slideDurationsMs).
  • Progress without waiting: the test drives the downsizing through a fake decoder and waits on settled(), not the clock.
  • E2E-032 after your OK on the built change: add two generated pictures, one of them a duplicate, check its place in the order, undo. Only with generated pictures.

Decisions

  1. Entry point: A, above the pictures.
  2. Duplicates: skipped, with a notice and “Add anyway”.
  3. Own order: new pictures go to the end.
