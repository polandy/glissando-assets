Picture length with music, on the beat

With music the user still chooses how long a picture stays — "about 5 s" — instead of the pictures always being stretched or squeezed to the music. Each change moves to the nearest beat; the slideshow lasts as long as its pictures, and a longer song fades out with it. New slideshows start like that; Fill the music keeps today's behaviour. Try both screens, the choice, the stepper, a long and a short song, and a song without a clear beat.

How it works

  • Picture length is a new choice of the slideshow, in the music editor and in the new-slideshow wizard's music step (which today hides the stepper as soon as music is chosen): About 5 s each (2–15 s in half seconds, the stepper that exists today) or Fill the music.
  • About N s each: picture k without an own duration would end at k × N; that point moves to the nearest beat (never leaving a picture below 2 s). Because every change aims at its own unsnapped point, the small shifts don't add up: 24 pictures at about 5 s last about 2:00. The slideshow's end lands on a beat too. Without a beat, or with Picture changes: Evenly, each picture stays exactly N s.
  • Song longer than the pictures: the music fades out (the automatic 2 s, or the own fade-out) as the slideshow ends — that already exists (ADR-0009). Shorter: the last pictures play in silence, as today when the share hits the minimum.
  • Fill the music is today's behaviour: the pictures share the excerpt and the slideshow ends with the music. Every existing slideshow keeps it; new slideshows with music start at about 5 s.
  • Own durations (picture editor) stay exact in both, as today. The same timing plays everywhere: player, video export, web page export; a .glissando file carries the choice.
Proposed (ADR in the PR): the choice is stored on the slideshow beside secondsPerPicture, not on the music — the length is a property of the pictures, and replacing the music keeps it. Existing records and older .glissando files read as "Fill the music".
