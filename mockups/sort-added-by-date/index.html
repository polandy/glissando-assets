Sort added pictures in by capture date

A slideshow of 12 pictures in its own order: the sunset of 8 July was moved to the front. Adding 5 pictures from a second phone. All pictures are generated; no real photos.

How it works

  • Only with your own order. A slideshow sorted by capture date stays as it is today: the new pictures take their places, and the screen offers no choice.
  • The choice sits on the Add screen as soon as pictures are chosen, under “Where they go”: By capture date (proposed default) or At the end. Your own order stays either way, and the slideshow stays “Own order”.
  • The rule for “By capture date”: each new picture goes right after the picture in the slideshow taken last before it (or at the same time). In the example the evening photo of 8 July lands after the sunset, at the front, because that is where you put the sunset. A picture older than all of them goes right before the oldest one. Several new pictures at the same spot keep their capture order.
  • The preview in “After adding” shows the whole play order as small thumbnails, the new ones outlined, and one line naming the spots: “2 after picture 1 · 1 after picture 2 · 2 after picture 6”. It follows the choice live.
  • After adding nothing new: the slideshow shows the new tiles outlined with “new”, and “5 pictures added · Undo” takes them out again wherever they went.
  • Server library (Immich, linked): the same choice; it only changes where the pictures go in the record.

Tests

  • Unit (pure addPictures in slideshow-edits.ts): into place after the latest earlier picture, ties after it, before the oldest when older than all, several at one spot in capture order, at the end unchanged, sorted slideshows unchanged. Plus the spot summary for the preview line.
  • Component: the choice shows only with own order, the default, the preview follows it.
  • E2E after your OK on the built change: add two generated pictures to an own-order slideshow by capture date and check their places.

Decisions

  1. Default: “By capture date” is preselected (proposed). Alternative: “At the end”, as today.
  2. Remembered? Proposed: no, every add starts with the default. A choice stays while the selection is open.
