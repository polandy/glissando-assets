Slideshow Details, simplified

Mood, look and music sit up front and never fold. Everything else is under Fine settings, which this device remembers open or closed (try it, then switch states). There are two layouts for the up-front part side by side. A tap on any setting opens its sheet, and the sheets themselves don't change. Pictures are generated gradients.

What changes

  • One form: every setting is a tappable tile or row with a chevron. The "Change" and "Edit" buttons, the "by mood" chips and the panel's switch are gone.
  • Follow the music's energy is set only in the Mood sheet. The Mood summary ends in "follows the energy" while it's on.
  • Picture times and Ken Burns move into the Mood summary. Pictures and Duration are already in the header. When the music runs shorter or longer than the slideshow, the Music summary says "11:20 of music". The Captions count is dropped.
  • Fine settings: Transitions, Portrait pictures and Trip chapters, followed by storage, link and TV, which stay as they are until the separate Share todo. It works the same at every width.
