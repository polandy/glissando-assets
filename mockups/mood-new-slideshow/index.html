Mood: a new slideshow starts on Calm

Three changes to the Mood sheet, compared against today's app (the Before/After toggle). Use Beat to move through the song analysis; the mood tiles and the energy switch inside the sheet are the real controls, live.

  1. Calm by default. A new slideshow's Mood tile reads “Calm”, not “Custom · automatic”.
